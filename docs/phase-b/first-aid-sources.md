# Emergency hub content: sources and validation status

**Status: `[SIM]`, not validated.** Every item of the emergency hub (migrations 0033 and 0036) was written in our own
words, in Arabic, French and English, from the open guidance below. Nothing was copied from another application. The
app shows each item under the amber "[SIM] content awaiting medical validation" banner until an authorised editor of
the Office's medical staff marks it as validated in the dashboard ("Emergency hub" → item → "Medically validated").

## Reference documents

| Ref | Document |
|---|---|
| IFRC | International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies, *International First Aid, Resuscitation, and Education Guidelines 2020* |
| ERC-FA | European Resuscitation Council Guidelines 2021, *First Aid* chapter |
| ERC-BLS | European Resuscitation Council Guidelines 2021, *Basic Life Support* and *Paediatric Life Support* chapters |
| ERC-DRW | European Resuscitation Council Guidelines 2021, *Cardiac arrest in special circumstances* (drowning) |
| WHO-DRW | World Health Organization, *Drowning* fact sheet |
| WHO-RAB | World Health Organization, *Rabies* fact sheet (post-exposure wound washing and prophylaxis) |
| WHO-CO | World Health Organization, *Guidelines for indoor air quality: selected pollutants* (carbon monoxide) |
| TN-CAMU | Centre d'Assistance Médicale Urgente (Tunis), national poison and scorpion envenomation guidance |
